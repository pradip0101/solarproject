About Us

About us
Since its inception in 2015

ADIAVINAYS Solar Energy has the capabilities to design, engineer, supply, install, operate and maintain solar plants (KW to MW scale), solar street lights, solar water pumps, solar water heaters and air source heat pumps. We’ve installed solar plants for residential, commercial, industrial, government, schools and non-profit customers. Empowered by a team of expert engineers, electricians, solar installers and sales professionals, SUS Solar Energy makes clean energy available at a lower cost than conventional energy like coal, oil, and natural gas. We’re a green energy company focused on offering turn-key solutions along with the highest quality of service and support. Our engineering expertise makes the switch from conventional to solar extremely easy for our customers and helps them considerably reduce their monthly utility bills. By revolutionizing solar energy and making it accessible and available to everyone, we are able to successfully harness the power of the sun and deliver clean and green energy to all. We have developed a highly economical, modular and easy to install solar system that delivers uninterrupted and affordable power supply to reduce your high electricity bills
